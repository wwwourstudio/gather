            How it works

            Video and audio travel directly between browsers over encrypted WebRTC. The gather server only introduces participants and relays chat. Up to six people fit in a studio.

            Hosting

            Whoever creates the studio is the host. The host switches scenes, picks who is on stage, and runs banners, brand, platform chat and the on-air AI. If the host leaves, the longest-standing guest takes over.

            Presenting

            Anyone can present a screen, a video file or a PDF. Cameras stay live, and the stage switches to a presentation scene until you stop.

            Recording

            Recording captures the stage exactly as you see it, with everyone's audio mixed in, and saves a video file to your computer. Nothing is uploaded.
