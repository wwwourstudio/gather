          How it works

          Video and audio travel directly between browsers over encrypted WebRTC. The gather server only introduces participants and relays chat. Each person can join with a studio code; up to six people fit in a studio.

          Hosting

          Whoever creates the studio is the host. The host picks who is on stage, the layout, banners and brand. If the host leaves, the longest-standing guest takes over.

          Recording

          Recording captures the stage exactly as you see it, with everyone's audio mixed in, and saves a video file to your computer. Nothing is uploaded.
