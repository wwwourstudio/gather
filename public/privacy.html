Privacy

Last updated 15 November 2026

gather is a browser studio for live shows and recordings. This page explains what it keeps about you, why, and what it never does.

What gather stores

  • Your account, if you make one: your email, nickname, profile photo and settings (brand, scenes, device preferences). Passwords are stored only as a salted scrypt hash. If you sign in with Google, gather receives your name, email and Google account ID from Google.
  • Studios while they run: who is in the studio, the stage layout and the studio chat. This is deleted automatically after the studio has been empty for a while, and it is never used for anything else.
  • Scheduled shows, if you plan one: the title, time and the guest emails you enter, used only to send those guests the invite and reminders.

What stays on your device

  • Your camera and microphone go directly between the browsers in a studio (WebRTC). They pass through a relay only when a direct connection isn't possible, and are not recorded by gather.
  • Recordings are made in your browser. They stay on your computer unless you choose to save them to your own Google Drive.
  • AI keys and stream keys are kept in your browser only. AI keys are sent to the AI provider you chose with each request and are not stored by gather. Stream keys go to the go-live relay and on to the platform you chose, and are never stored or logged.

Google data

If you connect Google, gather asks only for what each feature needs:

  • Sign in with Google: your name, email and profile picture, to create or find your gather account.
  • Google Drive (drive.file): access only to files gather creates, so it can save your recordings to a “gather recordings” folder. It cannot see your other files.
  • YouTube: to read your live chat and to apply the moderation actions you take (hide a message, time out or block a viewer) on your own broadcast.

Access tokens stay in your browser and expire within an hour. gather's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. gather does not sell this data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Other services

gather runs on Vercel, keeps account and studio data in a Redis database, may relay connections through Cloudflare when a direct connection isn't possible, and sends scheduling emails through Resend. When you use the AI producer, your request goes to the provider you chose (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or xAI) under that provider's terms.

Your choices

  • Use gather as a guest without an account.
  • Delete your account any time from Your account → Delete my account. This removes your profile, settings and scheduled shows.
  • Disconnect Google from the studio's Settings, or revoke gather at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions

Contact the team that runs this gather site at the support email shown on its Google sign-in screen.
