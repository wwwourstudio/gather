## Missing features

- **AI image maker outside the wizard.** Image generation exists only inside “Start a stream → thumbnail”. It can’t make a stage background, overlay or sponsor image from the Brand panel, and the AI producer can’t generate an image on request.
- **Media library sync.** The media library lives in one browser (IndexedDB). It does not follow the account to another computer and guests can’t see it; uploads to storage (e.g. Vercel Blob) would fix that.
- **Slide overview.** No slide thumbnails or “go to slide N” for PDFs. Long decks need a grid to jump around, and presenter notes aren’t shown.
- **Timed QR / support card.** QR and call-in cards stay up until removed by hand. Show-for-N-seconds, or auto-show every N minutes like the sponsor rotation, would match how shows run donation calls.
- **Banner editing on stage.** Banners can only be edited in the side panel: no click-to-edit on the stage, no per-banner colour/style override, no queue to step through a run of banners or tickers during a show.
- **Restreaming to platforms.** Going live on YouTube, Twitch, X, Kick, Rumble, TikTok or Facebook needs an RTMP relay (browsers can’t send RTMP). Today gather records locally and reads Twitch/Rumble chat; it does not push video to any platform.
- **Cloud recording / save to Drive.** Recordings download from the host’s browser only. No upload to Google Drive or Dropbox, no separate per-person ISO tracks, no cloud copy if the tab closes.
- **Phone dial-in.** The call-in line is a WebRTC link. Real phone numbers (PSTN) need a telephony provider (e.g. Twilio) bridged to the studio.
- **YouTube / X / TikTok chat.** Only Twitch and Rumble chat are connected. YouTube needs Google sign-in (OAuth); X needs a developer app; TikTok has no official chat API.
- **Durable bans.** Removing someone ends their session, but nothing stops them rejoining with the link except locking the studio.
- **Production checks.** No typecheck/lint/build pipeline exists (plain JS, no bundler), and the Playwright suites live outside the repo.
